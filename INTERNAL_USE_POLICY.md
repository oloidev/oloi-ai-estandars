# Internal Use Policy

This repository, its plugins, skills, agents, and related assets are for
internal OLOI use only.

## Authorized users

Authorized users are:

1. OLOI employees with approved repository access.
2. Contractors explicitly authorized by OLOI in writing.

## Authorized use

Authorized users may use these materials only:

1. inside OLOI-managed repositories, systems, and developer workflows;
2. for internal product development, operations, or services performed on
   behalf of OLOI;
3. under OLOI-managed credentials, repository access, and approval processes.

## Prohibited use

Authorized users may not:

1. use these materials for another company, employer, or client;
2. republish them in public or personal repositories;
3. distribute them outside OLOI without written approval;
4. retain working copies after access should be revoked;
5. remove ownership, confidentiality, or licensing notices.

## Access and offboarding

OLOI should enforce:

1. repository access through company-managed accounts;
2. revocation of repository, marketplace, MCP, and secret access during
   offboarding;
3. periodic review of active access for employees and contractors.

## Technical controls

Where possible, plugin functionality should depend on internal resources such
as private repositories, private MCP servers, company-managed credentials, and
internal documentation so that copied artifacts are not independently useful
outside OLOI.

## Legal note

This policy is an operational control document and does not replace employment
agreements, contractor agreements, NDAs, or IP assignment provisions.
